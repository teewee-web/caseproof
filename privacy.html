Privacy notice

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Solid Finish Solution is the trading name used for CaseProof. Privacy enquiries should be sent to solidfinishsolutions@gmail.com. The service and contact address is supplied during checkout and on transaction records.

Information we collect

We may collect names, roles, business contact details, payment references, project briefs, interview notes, recordings where separately agreed, draft feedback and approval records. Stripe processes card and payment information under its own privacy terms; CaseProof does not receive full card details.

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess project fit, enter into and deliver contracts, take payment, create and approve case-study content, keep accounting records, protect the service and comply with legal obligations. Depending on the activity, the lawful basis is contract, steps requested before contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent.

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed with service providers such as Stripe, email and website providers, and with editors or subcontractors specifically engaged for delivery. It may also be disclosed where required by law. Customer stories are not published without approval.

Retention and security

Enquiry and project information is kept only for as long as needed for delivery, support, disputes and legal or accounting requirements. Unnecessary interview recordings should be deleted after final approval or the agreed retention period. Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards are used, but no online system is completely risk-free.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact the email above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the service, suppliers or law changes. The current version will remain available on this page.

CaseProof does not sell personal data and does not use customer interviews to train public AI models.
